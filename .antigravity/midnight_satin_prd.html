# Midnight Satin ## Product Overview **The Pitch:** A premium reading sanctuary for romance connoisseurs, elevating the digital novel experience from consumption to indulgence. Users don't just read; they unveil stories in an atmosphere of intimate luxury. **For:** Romance readers who value immersion and perceive reading as a treat. They care about high-quality curation, visual beauty, and supporting their favorite authors through tangible value (credits). **Device:** Mobile (iOS/Android) **Design Direction:** **Tactile Noir Luxury.** Deepest onyx backgrounds meet metallic gold accents. It feels like reading by candlelight in a velvet-draped room. **Inspired by:** The packaging of Pat McGrath Labs, the typography of *Vogue* Noir editions, and the interface of luxury watch apps like Hodinkee. --- ## Screens - **The Boudoir (Home/Library):** Browsing current reads and curated "Top Endorsed" collections. - **The novel Detail:** Book summary, cast list entry point, and chapter list. - **The Reading Room (Reader):** Distraction-free text rendering with chapter unlock mechanism. - **The Cast Gallery:** Character profiles with illustrations and backstory. - **The Author’s Study:** Author biography, style notes, and bibliography. - **The Vault (Store):** Purchasing credits for reading and social endorsement. --- ## Key Flows **Flow: Unlocking a Chapter** 1. User is on **The Reading Room** -> scrolls to end of free preview. 2. User sees **Veil Overlay** -> "Lift Veil for 5 Credits". 3. User clicks **Lift Veil** -> Gold dust particle animation dissolves the veil, revealing text instantly. **Flow: Endorsing a Character** 1. User is on **The Cast Gallery** -> viewing "Duke Alistair". 2. User clicks **Rose Icon** (Like) -> Prompt: "Send a Rose? (1 Credit)". 3. User confirms -> Rose fills with gold, counter increments. If count > 1000, a Trophy badge animates onto the profile. ---
Design System ## Color Palette - **Primary:** `#D4AF37` - Metallic Gold (Action buttons, active states, key icons) - **Background:** `#050505` - True Void Black (Main background) - **Surface:** `#121212` - Cards, elevated sections (with 2% opacity texture) - **Text:** `#EAEAEA` - Primary reading text (Off-white for eye comfort) - **Muted:** `#8A8A8A` - Secondary text, unselected states - **Accent:** `#800020` - Burgundy (Error states, "Rose" like interactions) - **Overlay:** `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)` - Golden sheen on interactive elements ## Typography - **Display (Titles):** *Playfair Display*, 700 Italic, 32px (High contrast serif) - **Headings:** *Cinzel*, 500, 18px (Uppercase, tracked out 1.5px) - **Body:** *Literata*, 400, 18px (High legibility serif for long-form) - **Script (Accents):** *Pinyon Script*, 400, 24px (Used for "The End" or chapter subtitles) - **UI:** *Marcellus*, 400, 14px (Clean serif-hybrid for buttons/labels) **Style notes:** The app relies on a subtle CSS texture overlay (`url('silk-noise.png')`) applied to dark surfaces to mimic fabric grain. Drop shadows are tight and gold-tinted (`0px 4px 12px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`), creating a glow rather than a shadow. ## Design Tokens ```css :root { --color-primary: #D4AF37; --color-background: #050505; --color-surface: #121212; --color-text: #EAEAEA; --color-accent: #800020; --font-display: 'Playfair Display', serif; --font-body: 'Literata', serif; --font-ui: 'Marcellus', serif; --radius-sm: 2px; /* Sharp, elegant corners */ --radius-lg: 4px; --shadow-gold-glow: 0px 4px 20px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15); --texture-silk: url('/assets/silk-grain-dark.png'); } ```

Screen Specifications ### The Boudoir (Home) **Purpose:** Main navigation hub. Feels like browsing a private bookshelf. **Layout:** Single column vertical scroll. Sticky bottom nav bar. **Key Elements:** - **Hero Carousel:** 350px height. Large book covers with vignette edges fading into black. - **Section Headers:** *Cinzel* font, "CURRENT AFFAIRS" (Reading now) and "HIGH SOCIETY" (Trending/Trophy winners). - **Book Cards:** 120px height, horizontal flex. Left: Cover image (2:3 aspect). Right: Title (*Playfair*), Author (*Marcellus*), Progress bar (thin gold line). **States:** - **Empty:** "Your shelf is waiting." centered in *Pinyon Script*, gold color. - **Loading:** Shimmer effect in dark grey/gold gradient. **Components:** - **Bottom Nav:** Fixed, `#050505` background. Icons: Quill (Home), Open Book (Library), Mask (Profile), Coin (Store). Active icon glows gold. **Interactions:** - **Tap Book Card:** Transitions to **Novel Detail** via shared element transition (cover expands). ### The Novel Detail **Purpose:** The "sales page" for a specific book. **Layout:** Parallax cover image top. Content scrolls over it. **Key Elements:** - **Cover Header:** Full width image, fades to black at bottom. Title overlay in white/gold. - **Metadata Row:** "Romance" • "Historical" • "Spicy" (Pill tags, 1px gold border). - **Synopsis:** First 3 lines visible, "Read More" expands inline. - **Cast Preview:** Horizontal scroll of circular character portraits. Label: "The Players". - **Chapter List:** Vertical list. Left: "Chapter I". Right: "Free" or "Lock Icon". **Interactions:** - **Tap "Start Reading":** Floating Action Button (FAB), bottom right. Gold circle, black play icon. Ripple effect on press. - **Tap Cast Member:** Opens **Cast Gallery** modal. ### The Reading Room **Purpose:** Pure reading experience. Minimal UI. **Layout:** Edge-to-edge text. Controls hidden by default. **Key Elements:** - **Text Area:** Justified text, *Literata*, 18px, 1.6lh. Margins 24px. - **Chapter Break:** Ornamental divider (filigree SVG in gold). - **The Veil (Paywall):** At end of free content. Blurred text below. Button: "Unlock Chapter (5 Credits)". **Interactions:** - **Tap Center:** Toggles HUD (Header: Back button; Footer: Font settings, Chapter scrubber). - **Unlock:** Button press triggers "Gold Dust" particle explosion, blur removes. ### The Cast Gallery **Purpose:** Deep dive into characters. Monetized social proof. **Layout:** Full-screen modal swiper (Tinder-style but static until swiped). **Key Elements:** - **Portrait:** Full height illustration. - **Nameplate:** Bottom gradient overlay. Name in *Playfair Display*. - **Backstory Card:** Tap to flip card. Reveals stats (Age, Occupation, Secrets). - **Endorsement Fab:** Bottom center. Rose icon. Badge count next to it. **Interactions:** - **Tap Rose:** `-1 Credit` toast appears at top. Rose pulses. Trophy appears if count is high. ### The Author’s Study **Purpose:** Humanize the creator and cross-sell series. **Layout:** Standard profile layout with luxe touches. **Key Elements:** - **Avatar:** Hexagonal mask, gold border. - **Bio:** Short text. "Writing Style: [Tag] [Tag]". - **Series List:** Vertical list of books grouping by series. - **Trophy Case:** Grid of trophies earned by their characters/books. ### The Vault (Store) **Purpose:** Credit purchasing. **Layout:** Grid of "Coin Purses". **Key Elements:** - **Credit Balance:** Huge number at top, *Playfair Display* Gold. - **Purchase Cards:** Rectangles. - "Handful of Gold" (50 Credits) - $4.99 - "Chest of Gold" (500 Credits) - $39.99 - Highlight: "Most Popular" ribbon in Burgundy. **Interactions:** - **Tap Card:** Native OS payment sheet. - **Success:** Coins rain down screen (CSS animation).

Build Guide **Stack:** HTML + Tailwind CSS v3 **Build Order:** 1. **The Reading Room:** This is the core value. Perfect the typography rendering (`prose`, `prose-invert`) and the "Veil" blur mechanism. The atmosphere lives or dies here. 2. **The Boudoir (Home):** Establish the navigation shell and card components. 3. **The Novel Detail:** Connects home to reader. Implement parallax scrolling. 4. **The Vault:** Essential for the economy logic (credits system). 5. **The Cast Gallery:** Complex UI interactions (flip cards, endorsements). **Tailwind Config Notes:** - Extend colors with specific semantic names: `gold: '#D4AF37'`, `void: '#050505'`. - Add custom font families. - Add `backgroundImage` utility for the silk texture.
