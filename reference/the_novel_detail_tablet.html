Synopsis

A tale of forbidden desire and regal secrets. In the heart of the midnight palace, a young seamstress discovers that the satin she weaves holds the power to change the empire's fate forever. When the Crown Prince commissions a garment made of the legendary Midnight Silk, their worlds collide in a dangerous dance of passion and political intrigue.

Behind every thread lies a whisper of rebellion, and beneath every touch smolders a fire that could burn the entire dynasty to the ground.
