Chapter IV The Gilded Cage

The Gilded Cage

Midnight Satin • Vol. 1

The ballroom was a sea of velvet and diamonds, a suffocating display of wealth that made Eleanor’s corset feel tighter than the laces allowed. She stood near the periphery, a shadow in emerald silk, watching the duke from across the room.

"He looks dangerous tonight," a voice murmured beside her. Eleanor didn't need to turn to know it was Julian. He smelled of cloves and old books, a comforting scent in this shark tank.

"He is always dangerous, Julian. That is the point of him," she replied, her gaze never wavering. The duke turned then, as if feeling the weight of her stare. His eyes, dark as the onyx ring on his finger, locked onto hers.

He began to move through the crowd, parting the sea of aristocrats like a ship cutting through dark waters. Eleanor’s breath hitched. Every step he took was a promise, or perhaps a threat.
